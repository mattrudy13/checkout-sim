About the numbers

All made up but kept roughly realistic. A cashier scans an item every 2.5 seconds plus about a minute to start and pay; self-checkout is half as fast per item and sometimes needs the attendant. Each cashier or attendant costs $16/hr and each kiosk $4/hr. Mood starts at 100% and drops 8 points per minute once a wait passes 2 minutes; when someone runs out of patience (6 to 14 minutes) they leave their cart and walk out.

"Wait" is the time from joining a line to being called to a register.
